About Password Defense

Built independently by Stacked Software. Designed for people who don’t want to trust a server with their secrets.

Password Defense was created around a simple idea:

Your passwords should belong to you — not to a centralized service.

Many modern password managers prioritize convenience through cloud-first architectures. That model introduces centralized storage, recurring accounts, and telemetry systems that aggregate user data.

Password Defense takes a different approach.

Our Mission

To build privacy-first security software that gives users full control over their encrypted data — without requiring blind trust in a third party.

We believe:

Encryption should happen on your device.

Your master password should never leave your machine.

Security software should not collect behavioral data.

Users should be able to operate fully offline.

What Makes Password Defense Different
Zero-Knowledge by Design

Encryption keys are derived locally from your master password using Argon2id.

All vault data is encrypted using XChaCha20-Poly1305 before being written to disk or synced.

We use the well-audited libsodium cryptographic library to implement these primitives.

We do not store your master password.
We do not store encryption keys.
We cannot decrypt your vault.

Cryptographically, we are incapable of reading your data.

Offline-First Architecture

Password Defense works entirely offline.

No account is required to:

Create or open a vault

Store credentials

Generate TOTP codes

Run security audits

Export your data

Cloud sync is optional.

Your vault remains usable without an account or subscription.

No Tracking

We collect no analytics, no telemetry, and no device fingerprints.
There are no advertising SDKs.

Your vault is not a data source.

Subscription Without Lock-In

Premium features require a subscription.

However:

Your vault data is always accessible and exportable, regardless of subscription status.

If a subscription lapses, the account reverts to the free tier.
Encrypted vault access is never revoked due to non-payment.

Your data remains yours.

Security Through Transparency

Password Defense is built on publicly recognized cryptographic standards.

Our encryption design and zero-knowledge model are described in detail in our documentation.

We welcome responsible security research and independent review.

Security should not depend on obscurity.

Built Independently

Password Defense is developed and operated by Stacked Software, an independent software business focused on privacy-centric tools.

The goal is simple:

Reduce the number of places where sensitive data accumulates.

Instead of asking users to trust us with their secrets, we designed a system where that trust is minimized by architecture.

Only you can unlock your vault.

Security tools should not depend on advertising, behavioral data, or aggressive acquisition strategies.
